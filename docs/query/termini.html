Five events dated by something other than samian — coins, dendrochronology, a volcano, the historical record — and the assemblage each of them bounds. Every one of these intervals must contain its own terminus, because that is the criterion that fixed tau = 6 in the first place, so this query is also a self-test. margin is how much room the model had to spare at the nearer edge: Pompeii is the tightest, which is why it is the reference that binds. Velsen is marked withheld — it appears in the figures but was kept out of the criterion, because activity continued on the site and the terminus may bound the base rather than the assemblage.
